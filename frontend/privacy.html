Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026. This explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Sojourn ("we", "us") operates this website and its companion app. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service, and we never sell your information. This policy describes how we handle it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you join our newsletter we store your email address. When you send an advisory or booking enquiry we store the details you provide — typically your name, email, destination of interest, approximate dates, party size and your message. If you create a member account we store your email, a username you choose, and a securely hashed password, along with any trips you save to your wishlist.

The AI itinerary tool. When you use the itinerary generator, the preferences you enter (destination, dates, pace, interests and notes) are sent to our server and to a third-party AI provider to compose suggestions. Don't enter sensitive personal information there.

Automatically. Our hosting provider records standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) in server logs for security and reliability. We do not run advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and plan your travel.
  • To send the newsletter you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To provide member features — your profile, saved trips and your own request history.
  • To generate the itinerary suggestions you request.
  • To operate, secure and improve the service.

Where your data lives & who processes it

We rely on a small number of trusted providers:

  • Supabase — database and account authentication. Access is enforced by row-level security, so members can only reach their own records.
  • Netlify and GitHub Pages — website hosting and delivery.
  • Anthropic — processes your itinerary inputs to generate suggestions.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash — deliver fonts and imagery; by serving those files they may receive your IP address.

Cookies & local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. If you sign in, an authentication token is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. A service worker may cache pages so the site works offline and loads faster. You can clear both at any time from your browser settings.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, and then delete it on request. Ask us and we will remove your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

Children

This service is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about your privacy, or want your data removed? Reach us through the enquiry form and we'll take care of it.
