Wellness

Sleep is the whole programme

By Noor Haddad · 3 June 2026 · 7 min read

Ask people what they want from a week away and most say some version of rest. Then look at what is actually booked: early flights, late dinners, a treatment schedule, and a room facing a road. The stated goal and the itinerary are barely acquainted.

Sleep is not one component of a restorative trip. It is the mechanism by which every other component works, and it is the first thing a badly chosen room destroys.

What ruins it, in order

  • Light. Not the curtains — the standby LEDs, the corridor gap under the door, the safe glowing green in the wardrobe. Ask for a room with real blackout and then tape over what remains.
  • Temperature. Most rooms are set several degrees too warm and many cannot be changed. Ask before booking whether the room has its own thermostat. Surprisingly often it does not.
  • Sound. Lifts, ice machines, the service corridor, the bar terrace below. The floor plan matters more than the room category, and no upgrade fixes a bad location.
  • Dinner at nine. A late heavy meal costs more sleep than jet lag does, and it is entirely within your control.
A quiet, dark, cool room on the fourth floor beats a spectacular suite above the terrace, every night of the week.

The first-night protocol

Arriving somewhere new, do three things before unpacking: find and kill every light source in the room, set the temperature as low as it will go, and work out where the noise will come from at eleven. If any of the three cannot be solved, ask to move — on the first night this is easy and cheerful, and by the third it is awkward for everyone.

And the flying

Jet lag is largely a light problem and only slightly a sleep problem. Get outside within an hour of local sunrise on the first two mornings and most of it resolves; sit in a dim lobby recovering and it lingers for the week. It is the least sophisticated advice in wellness and reliably the most effective.

None of this is glamorous, and no property markets on it. But a week of genuine sleep does more than any programme, and it is very nearly free.
