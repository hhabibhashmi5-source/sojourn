Morocco · Riads & the High Atlas

Riads and rose light

A walled medina · a mountain range an hour away

Home/Destinations/Marrakech

Everything worth seeing here is behind a plain door, and the doors open for people who are introduced.

A riad is a house turned inward: blank wall to the street, and a courtyard of citrus and tilework once you are through. We take whole riads rather than rooms — five or six bedrooms, a cook, a rooftop for breakfast, and staff who understand that guests come here to be left alone.

The medina is best walked early and with someone who knows which workshops are real. Afternoons we tend to send people out — the Agafay desert for dusk, or up into the High Atlas, an hour away and fifteen degrees cooler, where the valleys still work the way they always have.
