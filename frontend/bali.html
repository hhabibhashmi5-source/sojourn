Indonesia · Jungle & Ceremony

Jungle and ceremony

Twenty thousand temples · two coasts · one very old calendar

Home/Destinations/Bali

Bali is two islands: the one on the postcards, and the one that is still, unmistakably, a religion with a coastline attached.

We split most weeks between the cliffs and the hills. Uluwatu for the water — a villa on the edge, a break below, sunsets that empty the terrace of conversation. Then inland to Ubud and beyond, where the temperature drops, the rice terraces start, and the days take their shape from the ceremonial calendar rather than yours.

Ceremony is the part people underestimate. With the right introduction you are not watching a performance but sitting at the edge of something real — a temple anniversary, a blessing at dawn, a healer who will see you because our fixer’s family has known his for thirty years. We ask that you go gently with it.
