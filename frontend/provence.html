Southern France · Countryside

Slow Provençal days

Lavender, limestone and a market every morning somewhere

Home/Destinations/Provence

The point of Provence is that nothing much happens, extremely well.

A mas with a long table, a pool under plane trees, and a cook who arrives with whatever the market had. That is most of it. We add the parts that are hard to arrange alone: a cellar visit with the winemaker rather than the tasting room, a truffle morning in season, a chef who will come and cook for eight without turning it into an event.

The villages do the rest — Gordes, Ménerbes, Roussillon — best taken one per day and abandoned by noon. July and August are hot and full; we push almost everyone to late May, June or September, when the lavender is right and the terraces are yours.
