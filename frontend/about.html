About Sojourn

A quieter way to travel — and the person who built it.

The idea

Sojourn is an independent home for quiet-luxury travel — fewer places, known deeply. It's built on a simple belief: that the best journeys are calmer, slower and more considered than the internet usually makes them look. Privacy over spectacle, presence over pace.

Here you'll find a small, curated atlas of destinations, long-form essays in the Journal, an AI itinerary tool to shape a trip in minutes, and a bespoke advisory service for when you'd rather someone else handled the details.

The person behind it

I'm Haroon, a web developer and aspiring AI engineer based in Islamabad. I'm obsessed with understanding how things work — not just building them, but diving deep into the mechanics and science behind the technology. Every project is a chance to learn something new, experiment with different approaches, and push what I know.

Right now, I'm studying computer science at Bahria University while building real applications and exploring the intersection of web development and AI. I love creating things from scratch — whether that's full-stack web apps or experimenting with emerging tech — because the process of making something teaches you way more than just reading about it.

My goal is simple: earn my way to the top through skill and knowledge, build financial independence, and become someone who leads and shapes what gets built. I'm driven by the idea of gaining as much as I can — experience, understanding, capability — and using that to create things that matter.

If you're here, you're seeing part of that journey.
