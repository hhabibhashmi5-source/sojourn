Norway · Fjords & Northern Light

Fjords in silence

A thousand fjords · a coastline longer than the equator

Home/Destinations/Norway

Scale is the luxury here. Everything else — the lodge, the boat, the food — is in service of getting you somewhere large and leaving you there.

In summer the light simply does not go, and the days lose their edges: a fjord swum at eleven at night, a hike that starts after dinner, a vessel that moves you between anchorages while you sleep. We favour small design lodges and a chartered boat over hotels, because the point is to be in the landscape rather than looking at it through glass.

The winter case is different and, for some, better. Above the Arctic circle from late September the aurora becomes a reasonable expectation rather than a gamble, and the cold makes everything — the sauna, the sea, the quiet — considerably more vivid.
