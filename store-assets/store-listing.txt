FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
Sojourn is a members' house for quiet luxury travel — a calmer way to plan
journeys that mean something. Fewer places, known deeply. Privacy over
spectacle, presence over pace.

Browse a curated atlas of destinations chosen for depth rather than noise —
private islands, cliffside coasts, alpine retreats and cultural capitals —
each with where to stay, when to go and why it's worth the distance.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The Atlas — a small, considered book of destinations, filterable by the kind
  of trip you're after: islands, coast, mountains, culture or countryside.
• The Journal — long-form essays on travelling slowly and well: shoulder-season
  strategy, conscious luxury, wellness, design and the culture of going deeper.
• AI Itinerary — tell us where and how you like to travel and receive a
  considered, day-by-day plan in moments, ready for an advisor to perfect.
• Bespoke Advisory — a dedicated advisor and a black book most travellers never
  see, so a trip is planned end to end and you simply arrive.
• Your account — save destinations to a private wishlist and keep your requests
  in one quiet place.

Sojourn is designed to be fast, elegant and distraction-free, and works offline
for the pages you've already visited.

No noise. A few considered notes a season. Travel, distilled to what matters.
